# Vorvex Anti-Goals

## Purpose

These are durable refusals. They define outcomes and behaviours Vorvex will not accept, even when they appear commercially familiar or technically convenient.

## We Will Not Become a Guilt-Driven Budgeting App

Guilt narrows understanding and ignores the circumstances behind decisions. Vorvex may explain spending and trade-offs, but it must not treat compliance with a budget as the measure of a person's worth or success.

## We Will Not Shame Users

Debt, loss, unusual purchases, and imperfect choices are part of real lives. Product language, alerts, recommendations, and comparisons must remain factual and respectful.

## We Will Not Optimize for Screen Time

Vorvex succeeds when a person leaves with greater clarity. Teams must not prioritize session length, daily return, or interaction count over useful outcomes.

## We Will Not Manipulate Through Dark Patterns

Artificial urgency, hidden consequences, obstructive cancellation, streaks, and deceptive choices weaken ownership. Important actions must be understandable and freely chosen.

## We Will Not Reduce a Life to a Single Score

One score cannot represent a person's circumstances, values, progress, or future. The product may provide measures for specific questions, but it must preserve context and explain their limits.

## We Will Not Sell User Data

A digital twin requires deep trust. Personal information must not become a commodity sold to parties whose interests are separate from the person.

## We Will Not Let Advertising Distort Recommendations

Advice and prioritization must serve the person. Advertising must not determine which options, risks, or actions the product presents as important.

## We Will Not Force Everyone Into the Same Life Structure

People care about different Worlds and define progress differently. Vorvex must not require one fixed set of categories, goals, or life stages.

## We Will Not Expose Complexity Merely Because It Exists

The system may contain deep detail, but the interface should show what matters in the current context. Technical completeness is not a reason to increase cognitive load.

## We Will Not Make Transactions the Primary Navigation

Transactions are evidence and history. People navigate through their lives, Worlds, Objects, and questions; transaction detail appears when it explains those contexts.

## We Will Not Let Multiple Views Create Conflicting Records

The same information may appear in several useful perspectives, but each view must refer to one authoritative object. Convenience in the interface must not compromise trust in the data.

## We Will Not Add AI for Novelty

An assistant or automated capability must solve a specific user problem and explain important conclusions. Novelty, fashion, or the appearance of sophistication is not sufficient reason to add it.

## We Will Not Let Documentation Substitute for Shipping

Permanent documents preserve direction and learning. They must not become a reason to delay an obvious, useful prototype improvement or avoid testing assumptions with real use.

## We Will Not Implement Deferred Ideas Without Prioritization

Capturing an idea does not approve it. Parking-lot items move into implementation only after explicit review, prioritization, and a scoped task.

## We Will Not Let an AI Agent Silently Redefine the Product

Agents may propose better ideas, but product authority remains with the founder and the approved decision process. Material changes to the mission, experience, or architecture require review and approval.
